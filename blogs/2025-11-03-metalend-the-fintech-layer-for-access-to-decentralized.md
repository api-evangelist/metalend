Decentralized Finance (DeFi) is constantly evolving and it’s expansion can make the process of finding and accessing yield opportunities quite daunting. The typical user encounters a maze of protocols, tokens, and interfaces, each with its own mechanics, set of risks and layers of management. This complexity is reminiscent of how traditional financial systems operate: built on opaque, intricate layers that remain hidden from most people.
