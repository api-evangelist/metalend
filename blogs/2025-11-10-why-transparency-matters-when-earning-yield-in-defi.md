Earning yield in DeFi should feel empowering, not confusing. There has been countless events in the ecosystem which have shown how important it is to understand where your funds are being deployed when you join an earn program. Many users in other protocols learned only after losses occurred that their assets had been placed into strategies or handed off to curators they never selected themselves.
