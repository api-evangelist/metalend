For as long as financial markets have existed, there has been a relentless pursuit of higher yields. Individuals, institutions, and entire economies have often been shaped by this chase. But history has shown — repeatedly — that when returns climb without clear justification, over-leverage creeps in, risks compound beneath the surface, and inevitably, bubbles burst.
