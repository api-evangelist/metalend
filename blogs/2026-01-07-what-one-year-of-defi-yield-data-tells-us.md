At MetaLend, we spend a lot of time looking at yield data. One of the questions we always want clearly answered is simple: How does automated yield rebalancing perform over a full year compared to holding a single lending pool? To find out, we analyzed lending performance across 2025 using a straightforward setup: deposit on January 1st, 2025 , and hold through the end of the year.
